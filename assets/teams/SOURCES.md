# Team Logos

Bundled team marks are from https://github.com/lootmarket/esport-team-logos,
except 1win, which uses the Steam team logo URL in the bundled OpenDota snapshot.
Original paths and matching names are listed in the [Matchcut Data catalog](https://github.com/Cass-ette/matchcut-data/blob/main/catalog/team-logos.json).
These are third-party team trademarks, not original Matchcut artwork. The collection
does not confer trademark rights or imply endorsement by the teams.

Matchcut Data maintains these local PNG defaults. The application uses a bundled
snapshot; no request to this repository is needed during video creation.
Custom uploads are stored separately and take precedence over these defaults.
